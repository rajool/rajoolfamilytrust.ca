Rajool Family Trust

What one generation builds, held for the next.

A private family trust, established to hold family assets for the long term.

Principles

The Trustees act with care, independence and a long view.

Continuity
The Trust is meant to last across generations, not market cycles.
Care
Its assets are looked after as if they were held for those who come next.
Prudence
Decisions are made slowly, with independent advice, and recorded.
Integrity
The Trustees keep their word and keep proper accounts.

Our mark

The mark is a Persian four-centred arch, the pointed profile in which the old bridges of Khuzestan, Sasanian in origin, were rebuilt, drawn exactly from four centres and cut out of a solid band.

The number of spans says who is speaking: the Trust has one, the first span; Rajool Holdings has two; Renova Properties has three, an arcade.

Notice

The Rajool Family Trust is a private family trust. It does not provide trust, trustee, fiduciary or financial services to anyone, does not accept money from the public and does not seek investment.
